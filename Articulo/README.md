# BioGraphBench Article Workspace

This folder contains article-facing planning and manuscript materials.

Current files:

- `paper_results_plan.md`: five-section structure and result narrative.
- `paper_tables_index.csv`: planned article tables and their source artifacts.
- `paper_figures_index.csv`: planned article figures and their source artifacts.
- `figure_captions.md`: draft captions for the generated figures.
- `figures/`: generated PNG figures.
- `seccion_resultados.md`: full Spanish draft of the results section.
- `seccion_resultados_biographbench.docx`: Word-ready results section with tables and figures.
- `build_results_section.py`: reproducible generator for the Markdown and DOCX results section.

The audit evidence remains in `../reports`, while this folder should hold manuscript outlines, table drafts, figure plans, and later article text.
